- **Audience:** Groups of friends, local residents, and weekend visitors in Wrocław and Poznań looking to book off-road quad or enduro rides without owning gear or holding a motorcycle license.
- **Primary Action:** Check live date/location availability and submit a preliminary booking request (`/booking`), or call directly (`+48 729 497 816`).
- **Proof & Content:** Real vehicle quotas and hourly PLN pricing, verified `@cross_wroclaw` rider reviews, real photos from client & `@cross_wroclaw`, explicit Wrocław/Poznań schedule.
- **Visual Direction:** Alternating warm asphalt (`#1C1D21` / `#25272C`) and crisp white (`#FFFFFF` / `#F3F4F2`) sections with racing amber (`#F5B700`, deep ochre `#946200` on light ink/text) action anchors, `Oswald` uppercase split-color headings paired with `Onest` body copy, angled 45-degree photo cuts, and diamond tread feature markers.
