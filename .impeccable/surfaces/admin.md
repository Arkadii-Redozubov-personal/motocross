# Admin Surface Brief (`/admin`, `/admin/login`)

- **Mode:** Operate
- **Audience:** Rental owner managing incoming booking requests, vehicle fleet & rates, Wrocław/Poznań schedules and closed dates, and site texts/media.
- **Primary Tasks:** Filter and confirm/reject/cancel bookings without double-booking assets, retry failed owner notification emails, update models/assets/PLN tariffs, adjust weekday hours or date exceptions, upload photos (`<= 5 MB`), and rotate owner password.
- **Visual Direction:** Restrained dark slate/obsidian workspace in `Onest` sans-serif only (no display font in controls/tables), consistent 40px controls, clear status pills (`NEW`, `CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`), and explicit conflict alerts.
